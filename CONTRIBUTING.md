# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful and current.

## What belongs here

Resources about **managing the risk of AI in financial services**: supervisory
guidance, standards and frameworks, model risk management practice, tooling,
research, and reading. The lens is finance (banks, insurers, asset managers,
market infrastructure, and their regulators), not AI governance in general.

A good entry is:

- **Substantive.** A primary source (a regulator, standards body, or
  maintained open-source project) beats a vendor blog summarising it.
- **Current or foundational.** Either recent (roughly 2023 onward) or a
  canonical reference the field still builds on.
- **Live.** The link resolves and points at the thing it claims to.
- **Finance-relevant.** Horizontal items are included only where they are
  routinely applied in financial services.

## How to add one

1. Fork the repository and edit `README.md`.
2. Put the entry in the right section, keeping each section roughly
   alphabetical or chronological as it already reads.
3. Use this format, on a single line:

   ```
   - [Name (issuer or author, year)](https://link) - one concise sentence on what it is and why it matters.
   ```

4. Use plain hyphens, not em-dashes. Keep descriptions factual and neutral;
   no marketing language.
5. Open a pull request describing the entry and why it belongs.

Removals and corrections (dead links, superseded documents, projects that have
gone unmaintained) are just as welcome as additions.

## Scope notes

- Commercial tools are included where they are widely used in the field; mark
  them as commercial so readers know.
- One entry per resource. If something fits two sections, pick the better fit.
